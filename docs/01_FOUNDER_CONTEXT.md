# 01 — Founder Context

## In one sentence

Project Rebuild is an AI-powered Decision Operating System, not a fitness tracker, calorie counter, or generic chatbot — it helps people make better decisions at the moments that matter most.

## The first user

The founder: 39, former BJJ competitor, now a busy professional and parent with demanding work, interrupted sleep, and reduced physical condition. Reported medical context: prediabetes/possible insulin resistance, elevated visceral fat, prior kidney stone. Wants to rebuild physical capacity and identity without competing with family time.

## The unit of the product

One decision. Not a habit, not a workout, not a calorie.

## Mission

Rebuild the athlete within — recover physical capacity, energy, and confidence, reverse metabolic decline, remain present for family, and become an active example for his son — without making health compete with family life.

See root `FOUNDER_CONTEXT.md` for the full vision, philosophy, operating states, decision score/gamification design, and success metrics.
