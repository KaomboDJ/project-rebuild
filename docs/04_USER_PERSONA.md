# 04 — User Persona

## The first user (founder)

- 39, 1.78m, baseline ~101kg, functional milestone 91kg, previous athletic range ~86–90kg.
- Former BJJ competitor; also practiced capoeira, parkour, handstands, outdoor activity.
- Reported medical context: prediabetes/possible insulin resistance, elevated visceral fat, prior kidney stone. General education only — never diagnosis, medication, or supplement guidance without clinician oversight.
- Sleep: usually 5–6 interrupted hours (young child).
- Work: 09:00–18:00, cognitively demanding, meetings; can extend after a family pause.
- Remote-work days: can usually protect a ~40-minute daytime training block. From September, likely office days: Tue/Thu or Tue/Wed.
- Family time is priority from ~17:00–18:00 onward.

## Identity

> Sou um atleta em reconstrução.

Wants to feel light, energetic, physically capable, and proud again; wants to stay present for partner and son; wants to eventually return to a martial art.

## Eating risks

Sweets at night, Uber Eats when tired or unprepared, large portions, under-eating early followed by evening hunger. Non-negotiable: *mesmo num dia péssimo, janta comida que já existe em casa.*

## Training direction

30-minute P90X3/P90X-style sessions as the main re-entry tool; Insanity Max:30 only when sleep/energy support it; walking/mobility as the fallback after poor sleep or chaotic days; daytime training preferred so it doesn't compete with family routines.

## Communication preferences

Portuguese from Portugal, direct and practical, minimal unnecessary explanation. Confirm whether planned actions happened; if not, motivate without guilt and reconnect to athletic identity and family.

## Target persona (beyond the founder)

Former athletes, 30–50, now busy professionals and parents who lost consistency to work and family demands and want to recover their athletic identity. They know what to do; they struggle to execute consistently. Their real goal is recognizing themselves again as capable and energetic — adapted to the life they have now, not weight loss as an end in itself.
